# Figures 1B

Every figure is one panel in one PNG without text beyond axes, ticks and a legend where one is needed, at the width the report places it at (the Width column: 80 mm one column, 170 mm both); this note holds what the report prints around them. Colours, line styles and markers of the methods: `legend_methods`. Generated by `make figure-notes` from the `captions.csv` of each folder.

### Distillation and score

| Figure | Width | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [distil_score](../../report/figures/1b/distil_score.png) | 170 mm | **Distillation score.** Do policies that distil well score well? One dot per distilled policy: its final score against the test top-1 fidelity of its 256-leaf tree (base fit, test states of its final checkpoint). Pooled Spearman rho 0.85 (p 2.8e-08, 27 policies), within methods n/a. The pooled rho (R106) mostly compares methods; within methods is the mean rho over methods with 5 or more policies. ### Distillation fidelity

| Figure | Width | Description |
|---|---|---|
