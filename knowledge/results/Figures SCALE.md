# Figures SCALE

Every figure is one panel in one PNG without text beyond axes, ticks and a legend where one is needed, at the width the report places it at (the Width column: 80 mm one column, 170 mm both); this note holds what the report prints around them. Colours, line styles and markers of the methods: `legend_methods`. Generated by `make figure-notes` from the `captions.csv` of each folder.

## Comparison

### S

| Figure | Width | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [scale_advantage](../../report/figures/scale/scale_advantage.png) | 80 mm | **Advantage at 1M and 1B.** Does a method's advantage over its base control survive at 1B? Gain in percent over the base control at each budget, 95% bootstrap CIs; on the dashed diagonal the advantage is unchanged, below it the method loses ground at 1B. Changes whose interval excludes 0 (points): sil -4.4. |
| [scale_scores](../../report/figures/scale/scale_scores.png) | 80 mm | **Scores at 1M and 1B.** Does the ranking carry over from 1M to 1B? Mean final training score of every method at the two budgets (log axes); a monotone rising cloud keeps the order. Gain from 1M to 1B: combo x7.2, count-bonus-ppo-rnn x7.2, count-bonus-pqn-rnn x10.8, dyna-q x8.1, ppo x5.4, ppo-rnn x7.4, pqn-rnn x11.9, sil x7.1, smdp-skills x9.6. 1B has 3 seeds, so the order of close methods is not resolved. |
| [scale_speed](../../report/figures/scale/scale_speed.png) | 80 mm | **Learning speed at 1M and 1B.** Does a method learn at the same pace relative to its budget? Share of the budget at which the smoothed training score reaches 90% of its final value, median over seeds (T8); below the dashed diagonal the method reaches it earlier at 1B. Later at 1B: combo, count-bonus-ppo-rnn, ppo, ppo-rnn, sil. |
