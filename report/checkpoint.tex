\section*{1. Problem, objective and repository}

\textbf{Problem.} Craftax is an open-ended survival game in JAX: 67 achievements across nine floors, a maximum reward of 226, and a scoreboard at one million (1M) and one billion (1B) environment steps. \textbf{Objective.} Compare three baseline agents (PPO, PPO-RNN, PQN-RNN) with four methods built on them (a count-based progress bonus, self-imitation, Q-learning over hand-written skills, Dyna-Q over a skill-level model) and a combination of the two best, under one protocol at both budgets, and ask whether the 1M ranking holds at 1B. \textbf{Repository.} \url{https://github.com/Pawlo77/rl-craftmax} (public); the README explains setup and execution.

\section*{2. Minimal experiment: environment, agent, measured performance}

The environment is Craftax-Symbolic; the baseline is PPO. After \texttt{make install}, a short run is \texttt{make train-ppo ARGS="--env Craftax-Classic-Symbolic-v1 --steps 1e6"}, and the experiment of the study is \texttt{make queue PHASE=final-1m METHOD=ppo} (10 seeds, resumable, every metric logged to MLflow, \texttt{make mlflow} to browse). Performance is the score: the mean training-episode return over the last 2\,\% of the budget as a share of the 226 points. PPO at 1M scores \scoreOneMPpo\,\% $\pm$ \scoreSeOneMPpo\ (mean $\pm$ s.e. over 10 seeds), and the published value is 2.2\,\%: the reproduction check passes (gap \gateGapOneMPpo, acceptance band \gateBandOneMPpo).

\noindent Score in \% of the maximum (count bonus on PQN-RNN omitted for space). \textbf{Result.} At 1M the progress bonus on PPO-RNN scores highest, but not significantly above PPO-RNN (probability of improvement \poiOneMCountBonusPpoRnnPpoRnn, Holm $p=\pHolmOneMCountBonusPpoRnnPpoRnn$); the skill methods are lowest. At 1B the order changes, with PQN-RNN level with PPO-RNN, and the rank correlation between the budgets is \scaleSpearman\ (95\,\% CI \scaleSpearmanLo\ to \scaleSpearmanHi). \textbf{Limitations.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Three seeds at 1B, so those scores are indicative and carry intervals but no tests; no tuning at 1B (the 1M settings are reused).
\item The 1M runs used a laptop and the 1B runs a GPU cluster with another software version; a rerun of the three controls at 1M on the cluster stack showed no difference beyond seed noise.
\item The 1B runs use two GPU classes; PPO-RNN scores above its published 1B value (a gap we did not trace).
\item The 1B ablations are still running; the progress-bonus SimHash variant overflows its hash table at 1B.
\end{itemize}
