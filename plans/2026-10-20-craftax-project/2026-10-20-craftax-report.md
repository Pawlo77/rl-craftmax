# Craftax report implementation plan

Status: in progress

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** a report of 3 pages including text, figures and tables, with the references after them (the course rule, decided 2026-11-01 in [[Experiment decisions]]; the 4-page decision of 2026-10-20 is superseded), in the Interspeech template in `report/` that presents the methods of [[2026-10-20-craftax-project]], the three controls (PPO, PPO-RNN, PQN-RNN), the results in two blocks (main and "uses hand-written skills") on Craftax-1M and Craftax-1B with honest statistics, the in-game behaviour, the decision-tree interpretation and the limitations, with every number generated from the MLflow exports.

**Architecture:** `report/report.tex` (already stripped from the Interspeech example, `\documentclass[cameraready]{Interspeech}`) holds the text; `report/numbers.tex` (macros), `report/tables/*.tex` and `report/figures/*.pdf` are generated by `analysis/latex.py` of [[2026-10-20-craftax-comparison-and-results]]; `report/mybib.bib` holds the bibliography built from the research notes; `make report` builds the PDF with latexmk. `report/template.tex` (the Interspeech example) was only the source of `report.tex`: Task 1 copies its formatting rules into a comment block at the top of `report.tex` and deletes it.

**Tech Stack:** TeX Live (`latexmk`, `pdflatex`, BibTeX with `IEEEtran`), `report/Interspeech.cls`, generated tables and figures from `wut_murl_craftax.analysis`.

Report build from the MLflow exports to the PDF:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  ML["MLflow store"] --> EX["outputs/metrics: final-1m, final-1b, combo-1m, combo-1b, tune-coarse, tune-fine"]
  EX --> RS["analysis.results: figures and tables"]
  INT["outputs/interpretation: complexity.csv, fidelity_vs_leaves"] --> RS
  INT --> LX
  RS --> LX["analysis/latex.py"]
  LX --> NU["report/numbers.tex (macros)"]
  LX --> TB["report/tables/*.tex"]
  LX --> FG["report/figures/*.pdf"]
  NOTES["research notes frontmatter"] --> BIB["report/mybib.bib"]
  TXT["report/report.tex (hand-written text)"] --> LM["make report: latexmk, pdflatex, BibTeX"]
  NU --> LM
  TB --> LM
  FG --> LM
  BIB --> LM
  LM --> PDF["report/report.pdf, 3 pages + references"]
```

## Page budget

Decided ([[Experiment decisions]], 2026-11-01): the report has at most 3 pages including text, figures and tables; only the references may go beyond them ([[Course project requirements]]). The generative-AI disclosure and the acknowledgements are not excluded by that rule, so they sit inside the 3 pages as two short paragraphs at the end of Section 7; the references follow on page 4. The earlier plan (4 pages of content, page 5 for the disclosure, acknowledgements and references, from the Interspeech rules for regular papers) is superseded. The layout rules of the Interspeech example are copied into the rules comment of `report/report.tex` by Task 1.

| Section | Pages | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Title, abstract, index terms | 0.30 | abstract at most 1,000 characters, no citations |
| 1 Introduction | 0.25 | problem, why Craftax, contributions as 4 short bullets (methods, metric families, interpretation, open release) |
| 2 Related work | 0.20 | Craftax and its scoreboard, the comparable blocks of [[Existing Implementations]], statistics practice |
| 3 Methods | 0.45 | the three controls (PPO, PPO-RNN, PQN-RNN) in 2 sentences; one short paragraph per method from its plan: idea, change against its base control, key hyperparameters; the skill methods marked as using hand-written skills; the combined method in 2 sentences |
| 4 Experimental setup | 0.35 | benchmarks and budgets, seeds (3 tuning; finals 10 at 1M, 3 at 1B), tuning protocol, eval protocol, statistics, hardware, software (JAX, craftax 1.6.1, MLflow) |
| 5 Results | 0.90 | T1, F1, F2, main block first, then the "uses hand-written skills" block; reproduction gate per control in one sentence each (pass, or gap and band h); in-game behaviour (F3) |
| 6 Interpretation | 0.20 | one sentence per method on its strategy, the complexity result; the fidelity-versus-leaves figure goes to the Space |
| 7 Discussion and conclusion | 0.25 | obstacles from [[2026-10-20-craftax-training-analysis]], limitations, future work, links to the videos, Space and dataset |
| Generative-AI disclosure, acknowledgements | 0.10 | two short paragraphs closing Section 7 |
| **Total** | **3.00** | |
| References | after page 3 | not counted |
